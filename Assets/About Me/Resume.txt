## Projects I've worked on.

I'm working on tools I wish I had before I started. Two of them are currently available:

Kleos - [GitHub ↗](https://github.com/ThoriaDevelopment/Kleos)

> This media management dashboard tracks every content creator in a community from a single dashboard. It shows who's actively creating content and how their content is performing without the need for spreadsheets. Kleos pulls data from YouTube and Twitch API's, marks which uploads belong to your server (using keywords or AI auto-detection), ranks creators on a leaderboard, creates shareable community boards, and alerts you when a creator reaches a subscriber count. It works completely offline once the needed data is retrieved and allows you to create separate profiles for different communities. It aims to simplify managing more than 30 creators on different servers at the same time.

Iustitia - [GitHub ↗](https://github.com/ThoriaDevelopment/Iustitia) · [Web ↗](https://thoriadevelopment.github.io/Iustitia/)

> A fully client-sided anti-cheat for Minecraft 1.21.11 (Fabric). It monitors other players on your instance and flags impossible interactions like placing blocks from too far away, phasing through walls, flying, or running backwards... 32 different fully fletched checks in a client-based anticheat. It only sends alerts in chat (unless you enable /IUS verbose ) and marks suspects with name tag prefixes. It never bans (obviously), reports, or sends data elsewhere. Think of it as a radar that silently gives warnings to you. I developed this not only to give non-staff members a tool to not false-hackusate other players, but also to demonstrate my ability to understand how cheats work at the packet level. ITS CURRENTLY IN BETA.
